# How Sam likes the site to feel (read before picking each day's change)

Sam wants a site that feels alive and premium, like the best SaaS and agency pages, not a text document. These are changes he asked for himself and liked; aim for more of this kind of thing.

## What he liked (examples already live)
- **Rotating words in headlines.** Hero: line 1 fixed ("We grow businesses with"), line 2 types and deletes words in yellow (AI agents, DM automation, websites that sell, Reels that hook, one system). Idea came from oxapay.com's "Crypto payment gateway for Business / Websites / Apps" typewriter.
- **"Comment or DM" as a living word** (2026-11-15): a gold pill with the word in black; "Comment" and "DM" take turns, letters roll up one by one (React Bits "Rotating Text" style). Sam chose this from 5 live options: A slide-down with underline (Magic UI "Word Rotate"), B letters blur in (Aceternity "Flip Words"), C gold pill (chosen), D bright gold that dims then flips, E black box.
- **Moving logo strip** "Built with the tools your business already uses": real tools we use, scrolling forever, pause on hover. Idea from oxapay.com's "Listed on" strip. Honest version: tools we build with, never logos that suggest clients.

## How he works, so work the same way
1. He points at a pattern on another site. Look at how good sites do it (oxapay.com, Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Framer showcases, and component galleries: Aceternity UI, React Bits, Magic UI).
2. Adapt it to our brand: dark background, yellow #FEC702, our real words and facts. Never copy their text, brand or assets.
3. Small details matter to him: a box or pill must resize with its word (no empty space), each word must stay long enough to read (about 1.8 to 2 seconds), nothing may jump the layout.
4. Motion with meaning: it should draw the eye to the words that make people act (AUDIT, keywords, the offer), not decorate randomly.

## Good next ideas in this spirit (pick one when it is the best use of the day)
- Make other key words living where they help action: e.g. the bot section keyword chips (PRICE / MENU / BOOK) as a rotating pill in its heading, using the same pill style as "Comment or DM".
- Numbers that count up when seen (already used in the proof strip): reuse where a real number is shown.
- Cards that build or light up step by step when they scroll in (like the audit page offer cards).
- A second honest moving strip only if there is something real to show (e.g. platforms a client page can be on: Facebook, Instagram, Messenger).

## Never
- Invent clients, logos of companies that are not our clients, reviews, numbers or results.
- Use the same effect everywhere: at most one rotating-word effect per screen height, so each one still catches the eye.
- Ship motion that ignores prefers-reduced-motion, causes horizontal scroll at 375px, or shifts the layout.
