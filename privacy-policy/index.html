Sayed Mosavi (Sam Foundation)

2. Scope

This policy covers samfoundation.tech and its pages, our Facebook page and Messenger, the free page audits, the Hooks library and its emails, the services shop and video calls, the LaunchPad request flow and the Sam Foundation Club. It also covers limited technical information needed for security and reliable operation.

Do not send us passwords, login codes, private keys, seed phrases or anything we do not need for your request.

3. Information we collect

  • Website forms: name, email, phone, company, service interest, budget range, timeline, current website, your message and any files you choose to attach.
  • Facebook and Messenger: your public Facebook name and ID, comments you leave on our posts, and messages you send our page.
  • Free audits: the link of the page you ask us to audit, and that page's public information (such as its name, bio, follower and post counts, recent posts and screenshots). When you open your audit page or tap its options, we record that event with your audit.
  • Referrals: the business name, link and contact you send through an audit page.
  • Hooks library: the email you give us to receive the PDF, whether you ticked the box to get emails from us, campaign parameters (UTM) and the time you signed up.
  • Testimonials: if you send us a video testimonial, the video, your name and email, collected through our provider Senja.
  • Shop and video calls: your order, email, phone number, the page you connect to our bot and the dates of your trial. If you book a video call, the time you choose and your email for the Google Meet invitation. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • Sam Foundation Club: your email, name, membership level, subscription status, trial and renewal dates and monthly amount. Your password is stored only as a secure hash, never as text. Password reset links are stored only as a hash and expire after 1 hour.
  • LaunchPad: wallet address, selected network, token configuration, transaction hash and confirmed contract address.
  • Campaign and security data: campaign parameters (UTM), a hashed IP address, browser user agent and timestamps.

4. How we use it

  • To answer your messages and requests, and to prepare and send audits you ask for.
  • To run our comment-to-DM replies and a small number of follow-ups after an audit. A person on our team approves audits and follow-ups before they are sent.
  • To send the Hooks PDF, and, only if you ticked the box, our emails (see section 6).
  • To process shop orders, connect our bot to your page, start your trial on the day the page is connected, and set up video calls you book.
  • To decide which requests to answer first (lead scoring).
  • To run the Club: create your account, take payments through Stripe, show you the posts for your level, and send service emails such as password resets.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure and free of spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent, Art. 6(1)(a): marketing emails, Google Analytics, and video testimonials. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
  • Contract, Art. 6(1)(b): audits and downloads you request, shop orders, video calls you book and Club memberships.
  • Legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c): keeping payment and tax records.
  • Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f): answering comments and messages, security, spam protection and lead scoring.

6. Hooks emails

When you ask for the Hooks PDF we store your email, your choice on the email checkbox, campaign parameters (UTM) and the date. The box is unticked by default. Only if you tick it, we may send you tips, offers and a request for a testimonial: one email about 48 hours after you sign up and one about 7 days after, and later at most one email per week. Every email has an unsubscribe link; after you unsubscribe we send no more marketing emails and keep only a note that you unsubscribed, so we respect your choice. If you do not tick the box, you only get the PDF.

7. AI and automated processing

Audit texts and some follow-up drafts are written with the help of AI (Anthropic's Claude) from the public page information described above. Audit voice notes and Club videos use text-to-speech voices generated on our own computers. Lead scores are calculated automatically. These tools help our team work faster; a person reviews audits and follow-ups before they are sent. No decision with legal effect on you is made only by automated means.

8. Service providers

We share information only with providers we need to run our services, and only what they need:

  • Hostinger: our server, which hosts the website, the bot, the Club and our database.
  • Zoho (EU): our email.
  • Meta: Facebook and Messenger.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Google: Analytics (only with your consent), and Calendar and Meet for video calls you book.
  • Senja: collects and stores video testimonials.
  • Anthropic: AI drafting.
  • Telegram: internal notices to our team about new orders and requests.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. Where they do, they use the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Public blockchain transactions are visible on the relevant network and explorers.

9. Storage, security and retention

Website, bot, audit, Hooks, shop and Club data are stored in a PostgreSQL database on our own server. Access is limited to authorized team members, and we use reasonable technical measures, but no internet system can guarantee absolute security.

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, for our records and for legal requirements. Stripe and we keep payment records as payment and tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see section 10).

10. Your rights

  • Access, correct or delete your information, restrict or object to its use, and get a copy of it (data portability), subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example with the unsubscribe link in our emails.
  • Tell us in Messenger to stop sending you messages, and we will.
  • To delete data we received through Facebook, follow the steps on samfoundation.tech/data-deletion/.
  • Cancel your Club membership at any time from "Manage billing" on your member page.
  • Complain to a data protection authority, for example the one for Lower Saxony (Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen).
  • Blockchain records cannot be edited or erased once confirmed on-chain.

11. Cookies and browser storage

The website uses necessary cookies or browser storage for form protection, campaign attribution (UTM, kept for 30 days), request access tokens and your privacy choice. Club and admin logins keep a sign-in token in your browser's local storage until you log out or it expires. Google Analytics runs only if you allow it in the cookie banner; you can change your choice any time under "Cookie settings" in the footer of the home page.

12. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. They are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

13. Contact
