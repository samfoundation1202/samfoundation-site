Where most people go wrong

A wick is not a break

Price touching or poking through your level means nothing on its own. You only care when it breaks through with force and leaves a gap between candle wicks.

    Rules that keep you in the game

    Risk first

    Why 3:1 works

    You risk 1 to make 3. Win 1 trade out of 4 and you are already back to even before costs. The fixed target also stops you from moving it.

    Same small risk every time

    Decide the amount you lose if the stop is hit before the session, and keep it the same on every trade. Many traders keep it at 1% of the account or less.

    Practice before money

    Go back over 20 past sessions with replay or a demo account and mark every setup. Only trade it live once you can find it without doubt.
